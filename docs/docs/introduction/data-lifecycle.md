# Data Lifecycle

Where a dataset goes, from the field to somebody else's reuse. This page is the map; each stage
links to the page that owns it.

```text
Field Acquisition
↓
Time Series
↓
Transfer Functions
↓
Survey Package
↓
Validation
↓
Review
↓
Publication
↓
Discovery
↓
Reuse
```

**Field acquisition.** Station locations, dates, instrumentation, sensor orientations,
deployment notes. Routine at the time, and often decisive when the survey is revisited.

**Time series.** The raw electric and magnetic field recordings.
[AusMT does not hold them](../interoperability/external-archives.md): they stay with NCI,
universities, agencies and project repositories, and the survey package records a persistent
identifier pointing at them.

**Transfer functions.** The processed frequency-domain products, and the core of what AusMT
manages. See [Transfer functions](../science/transfer-functions.md).

**Survey package.** Transfer functions organised with their metadata, provenance and citation
information into the unit AusMT publishes. See
[Survey package](../data-model/survey-package.md).

**Validation.** Automated structural and metadata checks producing `PASS`, `WARNING` or
`FAIL`. Listed in full under [Submission](../operations/submission.md).

**Review.** The judgements automation cannot make: ownership, licensing, embargo, collection
membership, provenance adequacy. See [Review](../operations/review.md).

**Publication.** The package enters the curated record and becomes discoverable through the
portal and the machine-readable products. See [Publication](../operations/publication.md).
Derived products are generated by the next build, not stored in the package; see
[Science products](../science/science-products.md).

**Discovery.** Users search by region, collection, survey, organisation, time period or
product without needing to know file names or storage locations. See
[How AusMT serves data](../interoperability/api-overview.md).

**Reuse.** New interpretations of old surveys, integration with newer ones, regional and
lithospheric studies, exploration, methodological work, student projects. This is the stage
that justifies the rest.

The lifecycle does not stop at publication. Metadata improve, provenance is recovered, new
publications appear, and derived products get regenerated by better methods. Those changes go
through [versioning](../data-model/versioning.md) so the record of what was published, and
when, stays intact.
