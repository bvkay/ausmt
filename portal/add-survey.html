Contribute a survey

Files first. Drop your EDI, EMTF XML or MTH5 transfer functions, watch the stations land on the map, then fill the short required section below. The optional sections help us publish faster, but you can skip straight to package and submit. Everything runs in your browser; your files and details never leave this page until you submit. New here? A worked example survey package lives at engine/data/sample-survey/ in the AusMT framework repository.

This page performs local pre-submission checks to help you prepare a clean submission. Final validation is authoritative in the AusMT submission pipeline, the gateway runs the AusMT validator on your package and a curator reviews it before publication; if the two ever differ, the pipeline result wins.

1. Your files

start here
Drop .edi, .xml or .mth5 / .h5 files here, or click to choose. Files stay in your browser.
EDI, EMTF XML and MTH5 transfer functions are all accepted. AusMT catalogues transfer functions and their metadata, not raw MT time-series data. If you send a station as both an EDI and an EMTF XML, the EDI is what gets served and the XML is kept alongside it. Files are checked locally now; the AusMT validator in the submission pipeline is authoritative during curator review.
Have a quick look that your stations sit where you expect. No conflicts were detected, so no confirmation is required.

The intended release date. Recommended for an embargoed survey. Even after this date the data stays withheld until a curator sets the level to open, it is a record of intent, not an automatic switch.
An email or role address a curator can reach to arrange release. Written into survey.yaml, do not use a private personal address you would not want published.

4. Was this survey part of a collection / program (eg AusLAMP)?

optional

Only if this survey belongs to a national programme or campaign (for example, AusLAMP). Leave collapsed if it is a standalone survey.

Surveys sharing a collection are grouped in the portal and MTCAT. Collections describe programmes or campaigns (for example, AusLAMP) and hold no transfer functions themselves, all data, metadata, and provenance remain with the individual surveys. Use the same collection identifier and title across all member surveys.

5. I know my metadata

advanced

Provenance identifiers, instruments, processing and data types. Leave these to the curator if you are unsure, server validation and curator review fill any gaps.

Start by picking what the identifier points at; the citation relation is set for you. The raw time-series collection at NCI is Raw time series; a GA eCAT or state landing page is Entire dataset; a paper goes under Related publication above, not here.

Package semver: 1.0.1 metadata fix, 1.1.0 added stations, 2.0.0 reprocessed transfer functions.

6. Check and package

What happens next: package your submission here, then either upload it directly with a submit key (when a gateway is available on this site) or email the packaged .zip to the AusMT operator (contact on the About page). The pipeline runs a ClamAV virus scan and the AusMT validator, a curator reviews it, and the survey is published. The local checks here are advisory; server validation is authoritative.
